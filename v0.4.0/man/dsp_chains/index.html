HPGe DSP Chains

A DSP chain takes the raw waveform table of one detector channel and returns a table with one row per event and one column per extracted parameter. The HPGe chain dsp_icpc and its variant for compressed raw data dsp_icpc_compressed compute the baseline, the pole-zero (PZ) corrected tail, timing points, energy estimators from several filters, the maximum current (A), the charge-drift parameter (Q-drift), late-charge parameters (LQ), pile-up and saturation flags, and an optional machine-learning quality-cut label. The pulser and auxiliary channels use the shorter chains dsp_puls, dsp_puls_compressed, dsp_aux and dsp_aux_compressed.

The layout of the input tables and the DSPConfig are described in Waveforms and Configuration. The individual filters are described in Filters, and the functions that compute single parameters from waveforms in Waveform Parameters.

Calling a Chain

out = dsp_icpc(data, config, τ, pars_filter; f_evaluate_qc = missing)
out = dsp_icpc_compressed(data, config, τ, pars_filter; f_evaluate_qc = missing)
ArgumentMeaning
dataA TypedTables.Table with the raw columns listed below
configA DSPConfig
τThe decay time of the preamplifier signal as a Unitful time (for example 450.0u"µs"). It sets the pole-zero correction. How τ is determined is described in Decay Time
pars_filterA PropDict with the optimized filter parameters of the channel (trap, cusp, zac with rt and ft; sg and mwa with wl; for dsp_icpc_compressed also dplms with coefficients and t_pickoff). Missing entries are taken from config.default_flt_param
f_evaluate_qcKeyword. Either missing (default) or a function that evaluates a trained classifier, see Quality-Cut Label

The input columns are

  • dsp_icpc: waveform, baseline, timestamp, eventnumber, daqenergy.
  • dsp_icpc_compressed: waveform_presummed, waveform_windowed, presum_rate, baseline, timestamp, eventnumber, daqenergy, t_sat_lo, t_sat_hi, deadtime.

The columns baseline, timestamp, eventnumber and daqenergy are the values reported by the FADC. They are copied to the output as blfc, timestamp, eventID_fadc and e_fc. Encoded columns are decoded first.

Processing Steps

The two variants run the same sequence. In the compressed variant the steps that need the full trace use waveform_presummed (the "presummed waveform") and the steps that need fine time resolution use waveform_windowed (the "windowed waveform"); see Uncompressed and Compressed Input in Waveforms and Configuration.

  1. Saturation. The samples equal to the lowest ADC value (0) and to the highest value (2^fc_bit_depth - fc_bit_depth, multiplied by presum_rate for presummed waveforms) are counted, together with the longest run of consecutive saturated samples at each end (saturation).
  2. Baseline. A linear fit in bl_window gives mean, standard deviation, slope and offset of the baseline (signalstats). The mean is subtracted from the waveforms.
  3. Quality-cut label. If f_evaluate_qc is given, it is evaluated on the baseline-subtracted waveform (see below). Otherwise the label is -1.
  4. Extrema. The maximum and minimum of the baseline-subtracted waveform, before the PZ correction.
  5. Tail. tailstats fits a straight line to the logarithm of the baseline-subtracted waveform in tail_window. The result is the tail decay time (tail_τ) and the mean and standard deviation of the logarithm. See Decay Time.
  6. Pole-zero correction. The waveform is deconvolved with InvCRFilter(τ), which turns the exponential tail into a flat top. Steps 7 to 17 work on the PZ-corrected waveform. The tail window is evaluated again (signalstats) to check the correction.
  7. t0. get_t0 applies a fast asymmetric trapezoid with the lengths kwargs_pars.t0_flt_pars and finds the first time the output stays above t0_threshold for at least kwargs_pars.t0_mintot.
  8. Fixed-length energy filters. Trapezoidal filters with (rise, flat-top) of 10 µs and 4 µs (e_10410), 5 µs and 3 µs (e_535), 3 µs and 1 µs (e_313) and 10 µs and 10 µs (e_101010). The energy is the maximum of the filter output.
  9. Rise-time points. t10, t50, t80, t90, t99 are the times at which the PZ-corrected waveform first stays for kwargs_pars.tx_mintot above 10 %, 50 %, 80 %, 90 % and 99 % of the maximum of the 10 µs/10 µs trapezoid (get_threshold). The drift time is t90 - t0.
  10. Maximum of the simple derivative. DerivativeFilter gives the current without smoothing. The time of its maximum is t_amax.
  11. Q-drift. get_qdrift integrates the waveform and compares two consecutive integration windows that start at t0, delimited by the first and last element of qdrift_int_length.
  12. LQ. For every [early, gap, late] triple of lq_windows and for each of the start points t80, t90 and t_amax, get_lq subtracts the charge in the early window from the charge in the late window.
  13. Optimized energy filters. Trapezoidal, CUSP and ZAC filters with the optimized (or default) parameters are applied. The energy is not the filter maximum but the filter output at a fixed pick-off time after t50, read with a polynomial signal estimator (kwargs_pars.sig_interpolation_order and sig_interpolation_length). The pick-off time is t50 + rt + ft/2 for the trapezoid and t50 + flt_length/2 for CUSP and ZAC. The maximum of each filter output and its time are stored as well.
  14. Current (A). The maximum in current_window of three versions of the current: the Savitzky-Golay derivative with window sg.wl and degree sg_flt_degree (a_sg), the simple derivative (a_raw), and the simple derivative smoothed by a_mwa_passes alternating passes of a moving average of window mwa.wl (a_mwa). Additional columns are written for the fixed windows a_sg_windows and a_mwa_windows. The maxima are interpolated quadratically around the maximum sample.
  15. Pile-up. get_triggers counts the intersections of a smoothed signal with a threshold of inTraceCut_std_threshold times its standard deviation in bl_window, with a minimum time over threshold of kwargs_pars.intrace_mintot. dsp_icpc applies it to the Savitzky-Golay derivative; dsp_icpc_compressed applies it to the presummed waveform after a 2 µs/1 µs trapezoidal filter with a minimum time over threshold of 2 µs.
  16. Current rise. t50_current is the time at which the SG derivative of the whole waveform first stays above half of its maximum.
  17. Inverted waveform. The PZ-corrected waveform is multiplied by -1. The maxima of the 10 µs/4 µs and 3 µs/1 µs trapezoids (e_10410_inv, e_313_inv) and the t0 of the inverted waveform (t0_inv) are stored; they flag events with a negative-going component after the pulse.

In the compressed variant the pole-zero corrected presummed waveform is additionally evaluated in the two windows auxpz1_window and auxpz2_window.

The waveform generator and the raw table of the uncompressed layout:

rng = StableRNG(1)
τ_true = 450.0u"µs"

function synthetic_waveform(amplitude; n = 8192, dt = 16.0u"ns", t_rise = 50.0u"µs", rise = 1.0u"µs")
    t = range(0.0u"ns", step = dt, length = n)
    x = ustrip.(u"µs", t) .- ustrip(u"µs", t_rise)
    shape = map(x) do xi
        xi < 0 ? 0.0 : xi < ustrip(u"µs", rise) ? xi / ustrip(u"µs", rise) :
            exp(-(xi - ustrip(u"µs", rise)) / ustrip(u"µs", τ_true))
    end
    RDWaveform(t, 1000 .+ amplitude .* shape .+ 3 .* randn(rng, n))
end

amplitudes = [4500.0, 5000.0, 5500.0, 6000.0]
N = length(amplitudes)
wvfs = ArrayOfRDWaveforms([synthetic_waveform(a) for a in amplitudes])

raw = Table(
    waveform = wvfs, baseline = fill(1000.0f0, N), timestamp = zeros(UInt64, N),
    eventnumber = UInt32.(1:N), daqenergy = zeros(UInt16, N),
)

The compressed variant needs a presummed and a windowed waveform per event. The following lines build them from the full-rate waveforms by summing groups of four samples and by cutting a window of about 30 µs around the rise:

presum_rate = 4

function presummed(w, r)
    s = reshape(w.signal, r, :)
    RDWaveform(range(first(w.time), step = r * step(w.time), length = size(s, 2)), vec(sum(s, dims = 1)))
end
windowed(w, idx) = RDWaveform(w.time[idx], w.signal[idx])

raw_compressed = Table(
    waveform_presummed = ArrayOfRDWaveforms([presummed(w, presum_rate) for w in wvfs]),
    waveform_windowed = ArrayOfRDWaveforms([windowed(w, 2500:4400) for w in wvfs]),
    presum_rate = fill(UInt16(presum_rate), N),
    baseline = fill(1000.0f0, N), timestamp = zeros(UInt64, N),
    eventnumber = UInt32.(1:N), daqenergy = zeros(UInt16, N),
    t_sat_lo = zeros(UInt16, N), t_sat_hi = zeros(UInt16, N), deadtime = zeros(UInt16, N),
)

out_c = dsp_icpc_compressed(raw_compressed, config, τ_true, PropDict())

Quality-Cut Label

f_evaluate_qc is missing by default and the chain then sets qc_label = -1 for every event without running any classifier. A function can be passed instead. The chain prepares its input in the following way: the baseline-subtracted waveform (before the PZ correction) is smoothed by repeated HaarAveragingFilter passes (5 passes for dsp_icpc, 2 passes for dsp_icpc_compressed; see get_qc_classifier and get_qc_classifier_compressed), normalized to a maximum absolute value of one, and collected in a matrix with one column per event. The function receives this matrix and returns a tuple (labels, decision_values). The labels are stored in qc_label as integers. Machine-Learning Quality Cuts describes how to train such a function with get_qc_ml_func.

# A stand-in for a trained classifier: label every event with 1.
toy_classifier(x) = (ones(size(x, 2)), zeros(size(x, 2)))

out_qc = dsp_icpc(raw, config, τ_true, PropDict(); f_evaluate_qc = toy_classifier)

Pulser and Auxiliary Chains

Besides the HPGe detectors, the LEGEND-200 data acquisition records channels that are not detectors. A test pulser injects charge pulses of a fixed shape and amplitude at regular intervals; its analog output is digitized like a detector channel and shows a step with an exponential tail. Auxiliary channels carry digital or analog signals of the data acquisition and the veto systems. Their waveforms are short spikes on a constant baseline. Examples are the baseline channel, which is read out by forced triggers and records the electronic baseline without a signal, and the muon veto channel. These channels have no pole-zero correction, no filter optimization, and no energy calibration from a gamma line. Their DSP output is used to select events, for example to identify pulser events and forced-trigger events and to check the trigger thresholds and timing.

The production dataflow selects the chain of each channel in the section additional_detectors of the DSP configuration. The section maps a channel name to the name of a LegendDSP.jl function. In the production configuration, one analog pulser channel uses dsp_puls_compressed; the baseline channel, the muon veto channel and a further pulser channel use dsp_aux_compressed. The section raw_fields lists the raw columns that each function reads. The functions take the same DSPConfig as the HPGe chains (see Use in Juleana.jl).

FunctionReadsOutput columns
dsp_pulswaveformblmean, blsigma, blslope, bloffset (baseline in bl_window), t50 (µs, time of half of the baseline-subtracted maximum), e_max (maximum), e_10410 (maximum of the 10 µs/4 µs trapezoid), and the FADC values
dsp_puls_compressedwaveform_presummedthe same columns, computed on the presummed waveform
dsp_auxwaveforme_max (maximum minus the FADC baseline baseline), t_max (time of the maximum), and the FADC values
dsp_aux_compressedwaveform_presummede_max (maximum minus baseline * presum_rate), t_max, and the FADC values

All four functions take a table with the columns baseline, timestamp, eventnumber, daqenergy and the waveform column of the table; dsp_aux_compressed also needs presum_rate. The FADC values baseline, timestamp, eventnumber and daqenergy are copied to the output columns blfc, timestamp, eventID_fadc and e_fc.

Pulser Chain

dsp_puls works on a waveform that has the shape of a detector pulse:

  1. The baseline statistics are determined in bl_window with signalstats and the mean is subtracted (blmean, blsigma, blslope, bloffset).
  2. e_max is the maximum of the baseline-subtracted waveform.
  3. t50 is the first time at which the waveform reaches half of e_max (get_threshold).
  4. e_10410 is the maximum of the waveform filtered with TrapezoidalChargeFilter(10u"µs", 4u"µs"). The waveform is not pole-zero corrected, so the trapezoid output depends on the decay time of the pulse.

dsp_puls_compressed runs the same steps on waveform_presummed. The presummed waveform is not rescaled, so the amplitudes are in the units of the presummed samples.

Auxiliary Chain

dsp_aux does not read the configuration. It determines the maximum sample and its time with extremestats. e_max is the maximum minus the baseline reported by the FADC, and t_max is the time of the maximum sample. For dsp_aux_compressed the FADC baseline is multiplied by presum_rate before it is subtracted, because the samples of the presummed waveform are sums of presum_rate samples. t_max keeps the unit of the time axis of the waveform; the pulser chain returns t50 in µs.

A Synthetic Example

The example runs both chains on one synthetic waveform each. The pulser waveform is the charge pulse of the example above. The auxiliary waveform is a spike of 0.3 µs width on a constant baseline. The table of the auxiliary channel has the FADC baseline 100.

puls_raw = Table(
    waveform = ArrayOfRDWaveforms([synthetic_waveform(5000.0)]), baseline = [1000.0f0], timestamp = [UInt64(0)],
    eventnumber = [UInt32(1)], daqenergy = [UInt16(0)],
)
out_puls = dsp_puls(puls_raw, config)

t_aux = range(0.0u"ns", step = 16.0u"ns", length = 1024)
